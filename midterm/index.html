Life in Algeria

Algeria is the largest country in Africa and is known for its beautiful landscapes, rich culture, and long history. The country has mountains, deserts, forests, and stunning Mediterranean coastline. Many visitors come to explore the Sahara Desert, historic cities, and natural places like Bejaia and Kabylie.

Visitors can discover breathtaking landscapes across Algria, from the blie water of Mediterranean coast to the vast golden dunes of the Sahara Desert. The country offers a unique mix pf nature, culture, and history that makes every journey unforgettable.

Facts about Algeria

  • Capital city: Algiers
  • Largest country in Africa
  • Language: Arabic and Tamazight
  • Famous for the Sahara Desert
